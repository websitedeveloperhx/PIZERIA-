Four things we refuse to rush.

  • Wood fire

    A 900° oven finishes each pizza in about ninety seconds, leaving a blistered crust and a soft centre.

  • Slow dough

    Mixed with little yeast and left to rest, so the crust tastes of wheat and stays light.

  • Crushed tomato

    Crushed by hand, seasoned with salt and nothing else. The oven does the cooking.

  • Basil, last

    Leaves go on after the oven so they stay green and fragrant.

Good pizza is mostly patience and a very hot oven.

PIZERIA started with a simple idea: make one pizza properly and serve it while it is still alive. Every dough ball is rested, every tomato is crushed by hand, and every pie goes into the fire just long enough.

We cook for tables, not for tickets. Order one for yourself or four for everyone, and tear into it together.
